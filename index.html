About Us

AATHREYA AGRO & DAIRY FARM

Aathreya Agro & Dairy Farm is a partnership firm run with a simple belief: good food starts with a healthy farm. With over 50 cows producing about 500 litres of milk each day, we bring pure, natural dairy from our fields to your family.
