About Aathreya

Rooted in farming. Focused on dairy.

Aathreya Agro & Dairy Farm is the partnership firm operating from Anandapuram, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Our farm is built around responsible cattle care, daily dairy operations and a connection with the land.

  • 50+ cows maintained at the farm.
  • Approximately 500 litres of milk production per day.
  • Agro and dairy activities under one farm identity.
  • Located in the Anandapuram area of Visakhapatnam.
